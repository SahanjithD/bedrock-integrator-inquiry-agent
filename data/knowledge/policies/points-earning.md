# Policy: points earning and adjustments

## Earning

- Members earn 5 base points for each 1.00 of qualifying spend on a stay.
- Qualifying spend is room rate and resort fees, before taxes.
- Tier bonus on base points: Silver 0%, Gold 10%, Platinum 25%. Round down to whole points.

Example: a Gold member with 630.00 qualifying spend earns 3,150 base points plus a 315-point
bonus, 3,465 points in total.

## Adjustments

- A representative may credit points for a completed stay when the accrual failed or is
  missing, with approval from a team lead or the support approval workflow.
- The adjustment equals the points the stay would have earned. Goodwill points are a separate
  process and are not part of a missing-points adjustment.
- One stay can be adjusted once. A second adjustment needs a new investigation.
- Adjustments over 20,000 points need a supervisor and are out of scope for this workflow.
