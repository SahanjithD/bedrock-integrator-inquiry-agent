# Policy: replies to guests

- Write in plain, friendly language. Two short paragraphs at most.
- State what happened, what was done, and anything the guest still needs to do.
- Do not include card numbers, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses or loyalty
  account balances other than the change made.
- Do not mention internal system names, batch jobs, error codes or the names of colleagues.
- Do not promise compensation, upgrades or timelines that a policy does not state.
- If the investigation found nothing wrong, explain what the guest is seeing and offer
  further help.
