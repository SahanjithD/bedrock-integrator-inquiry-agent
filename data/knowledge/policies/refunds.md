# Policy: card refunds

- Refund an incidentals capture in full when the folio shows no incidentals for that stay.
- Refunds go back to the card that was charged. Never move a refund to another card or pay it
  in cash or points.
- A refund needs approval through the support approval workflow before it is made.
- Do not refund the folio settlement charge for a completed stay. Disputes about room rate or
  fees go to the property, not this workflow.
- Tell the guest that card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on
  the card issuer.
